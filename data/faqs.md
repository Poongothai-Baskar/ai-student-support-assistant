# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?**
A: Submit a request through the Student Portal under "Certificate Requests" or apply in person at the Academic Office. Certificates are typically issued within 3 working days.

**Q: I missed an internal test due to illness. What should I do?**
A: Submit a medical certificate to your class advisor within 3 days of the missed test. A make-up test may be scheduled at the department's discretion.

**Q: How can I check my CGPA?**
A: Login to the Student Information System (SIS) portal, navigate to "Academic Records" and your CGPA will be displayed after each semester's results are published.

**Q: What is the process to change my elective subject?**
A: Elective changes are allowed only during the first two weeks of the semester. Submit a request form to the Dean of Academics with your current advisor's signature.

**Q: How do I apply for a hostel room?**
A: Hostel applications open at the start of every academic year via the Hostel Portal. Priority is given based on distance from home and academic performance.

**Q: Who do I contact for scholarship-related queries?**
A: Contact the Scholarship Cell at the Student Welfare Office, or email scholarships@college.edu. Merit and need-based scholarships are reviewed each semester.

**Q: Can I get a transcript for higher studies applications?**
A: Yes, official transcripts can be requested via the Academic Office. Processing takes 5-7 working days and a fee of ₹200 per copy applies.

**Q: What happens if I fail an arrear exam multiple times?**
A: There is no limit on the number of arrear attempts, but students must clear all arrears before the final semester to be eligible for the degree.

**Q: How do I report an issue with my exam hall ticket?**
A: Contact the Examination Cell immediately, either in person or via examcell@college.edu, at least 3 days before the exam date.

**Q: Where can I find placement-related information?**
A: All placement drives, eligibility criteria, and schedules are posted on the Placement Cell notice board and the college placement portal.

**Q: How do I raise a grievance about a faculty member or grading?**
A: Submit a written grievance to the Grievance Redressal Cell via the portal or in person. Grievances are reviewed within 7 working days.
