# College Academic Regulations

## 1. Attendance Requirements
- Minimum attendance required to appear for semester exams: **75%** per subject.
- Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for **condonation** through the Head of Department, subject to a valid medical certificate or approved leave.
- Students below 65% attendance are **detained** and not permitted to write the semester examination.
- Attendance is calculated from the first working day of the semester, including days the student was on approved leave (leave days count as absent for percentage calculation unless it is on-duty/university-representation leave).

## 2. Examination Rules
- Internal Assessment (IA) contributes **40%** and End Semester Examination (ESE) contributes **60%** to the final subject grade.
- A minimum of **40% aggregate** (IA + ESE combined) is required to pass a subject.
- Students who fail a subject must reappear in the **arrear examination** conducted in the subsequent semester.
- Malpractice during examinations results in disciplinary action ranging from grade cancellation to debarment for one academic year, as decided by the Malpractice Committee.

## 3. Grading System
- The college follows a **10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)** system.
- Grade bands: O (90-100) = 10, A+ (80-89) = 9, A (70-79) = 8, B+ (60-69) = 7, B (50-59) = 6, C (40-49) = 5, RA (Reappear) = 0 (below 40%).
- CGPA is calculated as the credit-weighted average of grade points across all semesters.

## 4. Re-evaluation and Revaluation
- Students may apply for **photocopy of answer script** within 10 days of result publication.
- Revaluation requests must be submitted within 15 days of result publication along with the prescribed fee.
- Revaluation is applicable only for theory papers, not for practical or project evaluations.

## 5. Fee Payment and Late Fee
- Semester fees must be paid within the first two weeks of the semester.
- A late fee of ₹500 per week applies after the due date, up to a maximum of 4 weeks, after which the student's registration is provisionally cancelled.

## 6. Code of Conduct
- Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and punishable under the UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations, including suspension or expulsion.
- Use of mobile phones is prohibited inside examination halls and classrooms during lecture hours unless explicitly permitted by faculty for an academic activity.
- Students must wear their ID cards at all times within campus premises.

## 7. Leave of Absence
- Students requiring leave for more than 3 consecutive days must submit a leave application to the class advisor along with supporting documents.
- Medical leave beyond 7 days requires a medical certificate from a registered practitioner and approval from the HOD.
